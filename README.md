# Customer Churn Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges faced by subscription-based businesses. This project analyzes customer behavior, engagement levels, and subscription details to identify patterns that lead to customer churn.

The goal is to discover the key factors responsible for customer cancellations and provide actionable recommendations to improve customer retention.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze customer subscription data.
* Identify churn patterns and behavioral trends.
* Evaluate customer engagement levels.
* Determine factors contributing to customer churn.
* Generate visual insights for decision-making.
* Recommend strategies to improve customer retention.

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Cleaning

* Checked for missing values.
* Removed duplicate records.
* Validated dataset consistency.

### 2. Customer Behavior Analysis

* Examined login frequency.
* Analyzed engagement scores.
* Studied customer tenure patterns.

### 3. Churn Pattern Identification

* Compared churn rates across subscription plans.
* Identified high-risk customer segments.
* Evaluated support ticket trends.

### 4. Data Visualization

Generated visual reports including:

* Customer Churn Distribution
* Subscription Type vs Churn
* Engagement Score Analysis

---

## 📈 Key Findings

* Customers with lower engagement scores are more likely to churn.
* Basic subscription users show higher cancellation rates.
* Customers with longer subscription tenure have better retention.
* Frequent support tickets may indicate dissatisfaction and increase churn risk.
* Higher customer activity is generally associated with lower churn rates.
